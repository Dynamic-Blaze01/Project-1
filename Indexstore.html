OUR STORY

Dynamicx is a modern clothing brand built for individuals who move with confidence and intention. We create more than apparel — we create a lifestyle rooted in bold expression, urban culture, and everyday movement.

Our collections are designed to balance simplicity with a strong sense of identity, offering versatile pieces that align with both your style and mindset.

Every drop reflects our commitment to quality, originality, and attention to detail. Step into the world of DYNAMICX and define your presence.

Wear the energy. Live the moment.

OUR MISSION

To deliver luxury-grade streetwear at accessible prices — crafted for the everyday man who takes his style seriously. Premium fabrics, timeless silhouettes, modern energy.

We believe style is not a privilege — it's a language. And every man deserves to speak it fluently. DYNAMICX exists to bridge the gap between high fashion and street culture, creating pieces that carry weight without demanding a fortune.

We don't follow trends. We build identity. Every collection is a statement — designed with intention, constructed with precision, and worn by those who understand that how you show up matters.

This is not just fashion. This is DYNAMICX.

1. INTRODUCTION

DYNAMICX exists to give you gear that hits different — and your data deserves the same level of respect. This Privacy Policy breaks down what we collect, why we collect it, how it's protected, and what control you have over it when you use the DYNAMICX app.

2. NOTIFICATION OF CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We review this Privacy Policy regularly to keep it aligned with applicable data protection laws across every market we serve — including the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023. As DYNAMICX grows, adds features, and adapts to new technology, our data practices may evolve. When that happens, we'll update this Policy and post the revised version here, with a new "Last updated" date.

3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We keep collection lean — only what's needed to run your account and get your order to you:

Account Information: Name, email, and password (stored encrypted).

Contact Details: Phone number and delivery address, entered at checkout or saved to your profile.

Order Data: Items purchased, sizes, colours, quantities, and transaction amounts.

App Usage Data: Pages viewed, cart and wishlist activity, and in-app actions. This is stored locally on your device via localStorage/sessionStorage and is not transmitted to external servers at this time.

Notification Preferences: Whether you've opted into SMS or email updates.

Payment Information: Payments are processed directly by our third-party payment provider — DYNAMICX does not collect or store your card details.

Device Information: Where relevant, basic device/browser type, used only to keep the app running smoothly.

4. COOKIES & LOCAL STORAGE

We currently use localStorage and sessionStorage — not cookies — to remember your cart, wishlist, account preferences, and notification settings between visits. This data stays on your device.

If we introduce cookies or similar tracking technologies in future (for example, for analytics), we'll update this Policy and request your consent where required by law. No third-party advertising trackers are used today.

5. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Your information helps us:

• Process and fulfil your orders

• Understand how the app is used so we can improve it
• Authenticate your account and verify your identity
• Detect and prevent fraud or misuse
• Provide customer support and respond to enquiries
• Process payments for your orders

• For any other purpose with your consent

By creating an account and using the DYNAMICX app, you consent to the collection and processing of your personal information — including your name, contact details, order history, and related information — for the purposes described in this Policy.

7. LAWFUL BASIS FOR PROCESSING DATA

We process your data based on: your consent, the need to fulfil our contractual obligations to you (such as delivering your order), compliance with our legal obligations, and our legitimate business interests. Where processing relies on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

8. HOW WE COLLECT YOUR INFORMATION

We collect information directly from you — when you create an account, place an order, contact customer support, or otherwise interact with the app. We do not currently combine this with information from third-party sources, but if that changes, this Policy will be updated.
